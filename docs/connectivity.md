# Connectivity

## VERIFIED

### NFC and secure element

- ST21NFC binds at I2C `6-0008` through module `st21nfc`.
- Firmware includes `st21nfc_fw.bin` and `st21nfc_fw7.bin`.
- Stock uses `android.hardware.nfc.INfc/default` and the ST NFC NCI library.
- Secure-element services expose SIM1 and SIM2 instances.

### MediaTek integrated connectivity

Stock images and services account for:

- `connsys_wifi`, `connsys_bt` and `connsys_gnss` firmware partitions;
- MediaTek Wi-Fi and Bluetooth HAL/services;
- MediaTek GNSS AIDL service and LBS support;
- modem, `md1dsp`, `md1arm7` and `md3img` firmware;
- AIDL radio/config/data/messaging/modem/network/SIM/voice services and MTK
  extension interfaces;
- dual-SIM secure-element/radio configuration.

## UNKNOWN / hardware validation

- Wi-Fi bands, hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct and suspend behavior.
- Bluetooth classic/LE, audio offload, calls and coexistence.
- GNSS cold/warm start and assisted-GNSS behavior.
- NFC polling, HCE, UICC routing and payment-sensitive behavior.
- SIM detection, calls, SMS, data, 5G/LTE and IMS/VoLTE/VoWiFi.

NV, modem identity and calibration partitions are device-unique inputs. They
must never be wiped, copied into the repository or generated by extraction.
